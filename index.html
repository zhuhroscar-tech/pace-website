The problem

Generic timers weren't built for how attention actually works.

A fixed 25-minute Pomodoro doesn't know if you're deep in flow or already drifting. Most tools either ignore that difference, or go the other direction — clinical trackers that feel like surveillance. Pace sits in between: responsive, but never invasive.

Why Pace, specifically

Not a stricter timer. Not a medical app. Something in between.

Rigid timers

Pomodoro & countdown apps

One-size-fits-all intervals that don't know whether you're in flow or stuck.

  • Same rhythm regardless of how the session is going
  • Breaks you interrupts flow just as often as it prevents burnout
Pace

Adaptive, honest, private

Reads a simple signal you control and adjusts in real time — quiet when you're focused, supportive when you're not.

  • Adjusts prompts, audio, and breaks to your real session
  • No wearables, no camera, no medical framing
  • Session data stays on your device by default
Clinical / medical tools

Diagnostic & treatment apps

Serve a real purpose, but require clinical oversight and aren't built for a quick study session.

  • Not something Pace claims or replaces
  • Pace is a study tool, not a substitute for care

How it works

Three steps, every session.

1

Set a task and estimate

Add what you're working on and roughly how long it'll take. Pace uses this as a starting point, not a hard rule.

2

Check in as you go

A quick, optional signal — focused, drifting, or fading — lets Pace know how the session is actually feeling.

3

Get a real end-of-session report

See how your focus moved through the session and what helped, so next time starts a little easier.

Privacy, specifically

Not "we care about privacy." Here's exactly what that means.

Local-first by default

Session data lives in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server unless you explicitly export or sync it.

No login for the demo

You can try the full session flow above without creating an account or handing over an email.

No wearables, no camera

Attention signals are a check-in you control — never passive biometric tracking.

Never sold, never advertised against

We're not an ad-supported product. Your study habits are not the product.
